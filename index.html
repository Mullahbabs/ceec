About CEEC

A structured community for Cross River’s creative industry.

The Creative, Entertainment & Events Community — Cross River State (CEEC - CRS) is a structured and inclusive community bringing together creatives, entertainers, event professionals, influencers, promoters and other stakeholders within Cross River State.

CEEC seeks to move young people and industry stakeholders beyond influence and visibility into meaningful participation in issues that affect their communities and the future of Cross River State.

  1. 01

    Why CEEC Was Created

    To give Cross River’s creative and entertainment industry a single, organised and responsible community structure.

  2. 02

    What CEEC Represents

    Unity, responsibility, creativity, peace and constructive participation in civic and community life.

  3. 03

    Who CEEC Serves

    Creatives, entertainers, event professionals, influencers, promoters, media personalities, entrepreneurs and emerging talents.

  4. 04

    Where CEEC Is Headed

    A sustainable, transparent and well-coordinated network active across relevant areas of Cross River State.

Our Aim

To build a structured, inclusive and responsible community of creatives, entertainers, event professionals, influencers, promoters and other stakeholders within Cross River State — leveraging the power of creativity, entertainment and events to promote civic awareness, youth participation, social responsibility, peaceful engagement and positive community development.

The Community seeks to create a platform where young people and industry stakeholders can move beyond influence and visibility into meaningful participation in issues that affect their communities and the future of Cross River State.
